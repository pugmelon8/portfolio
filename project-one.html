The Friend?

A stop motion project about a puffer fish slowly realising that the new friend it made was actually a plastic bag, which is part of the reason for the destruction of its home.

Stop Motion

Project Overview

The story follows a pufferfish that lives in the vibrant and beautiful sea. One day, it sees a new ‘friend’ floating around the sea, so the pufferfish decides to befriend it. The pufferfish shows the friend around the vibrant underwater world. Unknowingly, the pufferfish’s new friend is actually a plastic bottle that was thrown into the ocean. In the end, we see the once vibrant corals and ocean environment are now dull and lifeless as well as fully trashed with plastic. The pufferfish holds onto the plastic as it sinks, while a plastic bag slowly covers the frame. The final message reads: Still think it is just trash?'.
